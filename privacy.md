# RC Bench — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 November 2026

RC Bench ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and optional display name. As you use the app we store the RC setup data you enter (differential oils, shock settings, tyre choices, track conditions, etc.), your subscription status, and basic usage analytics such as feature access counts.

We also collect device information such as device type, operating system version, and a unique device identifier for analytics and troubleshooting purposes.

We do not collect precise GPS coordinates. If you grant location permission, we use your approximate location solely to fetch local weather data for track condition recommendations.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your setup data is used to provide personalised AI recommendations, store and retrieve your setups, and enable team sharing features you opt into. Usage analytics help us understand which features are most valuable so we can improve the app. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. AI-Powered Features and Data Processing

RC Bench uses AI-powered features to provide personalised setup recommendations, chat responses, and analytical suggestions. To provide these features, we send certain data to our AI service provider.

**AI Service Provider:** OpenAI

Data sent to OpenAI includes your setup parameters (suspension settings, differential oils, tyre choices, spring rates, etc.), track conditions, and profile information (skill level, years of experience). Your email address is NOT sent to OpenAI.

OpenAI processes this data solely to generate responses and, under their API data usage policy, does not use API inputs or outputs to train their models. For more information, see OpenAI's privacy policy.

By using AI features in the App, you consent to this data being processed by OpenAI as described above.

## 4. Third-Party Services

RC Bench uses the following third-party services:

- **OpenAI** — AI-powered setup recommendations and chat features
- **Supabase** — authentication and database hosting
- **RevenueCat** — subscription management and in-app purchases
- **Expo / React Native** — app framework and push notifications
- **Resend** — sends account and help emails, such as tips for getting started. It receives your email address, the email's content and an account ID used in the unsubscribe link. Every help email has an unsubscribe link.

Each service processes data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We only share the minimum data required for each service to function.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using platform-native secure storage.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data and setups are retained for as long as your account is active.

### How to delete your account

**From within the app:**
1. Open RC Bench
2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom-right)
3. Scroll to "Delete Account"
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

**Without the app** (e.g. if you have uninstalled it):
Email [support@rcbench.app](mailto:support@rcbench.app) with the subject "Account deletion request" from the email address tied to your account. We will process the deletion within 30 days.

### What is deleted
- Account credentials, email address, and display name
- All saved setups, AI chat history, and uploaded photos
- Team memberships and content you have shared with teams
- Subscription state in our records (Apple and Google retain their own purchase records, which you can manage in your device's Subscriptions settings)

### What is retained
- Anonymized analytics events with no link to your identity
- Transaction records required to comply with tax law (typically retained for up to 7 years)
- Records under active legal hold or required by court order, if any

Personal data is permanently removed within 30 days of your deletion request.


## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

**For all users:**
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your setup data from within the app

**For EU/EEA users (under GDPR):**
- Right to restrict processing of your data
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent at any time
- Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

**For California users (under CCPA/CPRA):**
- Right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used
- Right to request deletion of personal information
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [support@rcbench.app](mailto:support@rcbench.app). We will respond to your request within 30 days.

## 8. Children's Privacy

RC Bench is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us at [support@rcbench.app](mailto:support@rcbench.app) and we will promptly delete it.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers OpenAI and Supabase process data. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country of residence. By using the App, you consent to these transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers in accordance with applicable law.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
